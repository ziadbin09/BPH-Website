About BPH

Psychology made human.

We believe psychological wellbeing grows when people have the language, knowledge and community to understand what they are experiencing.

BPH

A youth-focused platform bringing psychological education, emotional wellbeing and accessible conversations into spaces where people already learn, connect and grow.

Buraaq Psychological Hub is built around a simple idea: mental health conversations should not begin only when a crisis happens. Awareness, emotional literacy and early understanding can help people recognise patterns, seek appropriate support and build resilience.

Through workshops, masterclasses, fellowships, educational resources and community initiatives, BPH creates practical opportunities for young people and communities to learn about themselves and others.

Our work focuses on making psychological concepts understandable, relatable and actionable—without reducing complex human experiences to labels or stereotypes.

Our Direction

What BPH stands for.

Our Mission

To promote psychological awareness, emotional literacy and wellbeing by creating accessible learning experiences, conversations and community initiatives that help people understand themselves and navigate life with greater resilience.

Our Vision

To contribute to a society where talking about mental health is normal, psychological knowledge is accessible, and individuals feel empowered to recognise their needs and seek support when needed.

Our Initiatives

Learning that starts conversations.

Selected BPH initiatives conducted to date, including the Wellness Wednesdays workshop series and its first sessions.

Wellness Wednesdays • Session #01

Decoding Your Emotions

An emotional intelligence workshop designed to help participants understand emotions, identify triggers and develop greater emotional awareness.

Key takeaways: Emotion Wheel exploration, recognising emotional triggers, managing difficult emotions, building emotional intelligence and strengthening self-awareness.
Wellness Wednesdays • Session #02

The “Silent Crisis” of Normative Male Alexithymia & Male-Depressive Symptoms

A focused awareness workshop exploring how social expectations around masculinity can affect emotional expression, emotional awareness and the way depressive experiences are recognised.

Key takeaways: Understand alexithymia, examine norms around male emotional expression, recognise less-visible depressive symptoms and encourage healthier emotional communication.
Masterclass

Turning Fear Into Power

A practical masterclass centred on reframing fear, understanding its psychological function and using awareness to move from avoidance toward purposeful action.

Key takeaways: Understand fear responses, identify fear-driven patterns, challenge limiting beliefs and turn difficult emotions into informed, constructive action.
Series

Wellness Wednesdays

BPH’s recurring emotional wellbeing workshop series bringing psychology into practical, accessible conversations through themed sessions and interactive learning.

Key takeaway: A continuing space for emotional literacy, psychological awareness, reflection and community learning.
